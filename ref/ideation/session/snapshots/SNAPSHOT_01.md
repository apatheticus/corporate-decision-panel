## State of the Conversation

The Free Thinker opened with five directions for exploring the Team of Teams Agent Skill concept. The Grounder performed a sharp triage, selecting three for active exploration and parking two.

## What's Active

1. **Cascading Delegation Architecture** — The structural heart of the concept. The two-layer org hierarchy is not just flavor; it is the mechanism by which analysis is produced. An executive doesn't hold an opinion — an executive commands an organization that produces analysis. The spec must capture this distinction.

2. **Engineered Dissent** — The quality differentiator. Without built-in structural disagreement (CISO says no, CFO finds costs, COO questions executability), the system collapses into "seven agents agreeing politely." The question is where dissent is engineered: in prompts, in architecture, or in process phases.

3. **Decision Records as Product** — The output layer. The decision record is not formatting — it is the deliverable. Fractal deliberation produces fractal documentation.

## What's Parked

- **Institutional Memory** — Good idea, bad scope. Persistence adds storage/retrieval/staleness concerns that the concept seed doesn't call for. Parked as future enhancement.
- **Company Parameterization** — Table stakes. Will be included but doesn't need brainstorm time.

## Emerging Shape

The Grounder has identified a potential "core specification triad": architecture (how delegation cascades), tension (how disagreement is engineered), and output (what the skill actually delivers). These three threads, explored together, could form the backbone of the spec.

## Key Meta-Insight

The skill should be "a better version" of organizational decision-making — preserving productive structural tensions (CISO vs. Sales, CFO vs. CTO) while stripping dysfunction (politics, information asymmetry, ego).

## What Happens Next

The Grounder has directed the Free Thinker to push deeper on the cascade mechanics and engineered dissent together. Expect Round 2 to explore step-by-step process flow and where dissent is structurally embedded.
