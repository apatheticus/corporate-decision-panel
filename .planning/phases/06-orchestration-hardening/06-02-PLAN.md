Add CSO timeout handling with gap reporting and C-suite confidence caveats for incomplete research across the orchestration protocol, CSO agent, and all 8 C-suite agent files.

Purpose: When CSO Phase 1.5 research exceeds its maxTurns limit, the system currently has no graceful degradation -- the CSO either completes or fails silently. Output: Updated cso.md (maxTurns + timeout behavior), updated orchestration-protocol.md (timeout policy + RESEARCH STATUS flag), updated all 8 C-suite agent files (conditional Research Basis field + RESEARCH CAVEAT paragraph).

**Part A: Update CSO agent (agents/c-suite/cso.md)**

1. Add `maxTurns: 25` to the CSO YAML frontmatter (after `model: sonnet`). This value is generous for normal operation (dispatching 5 team leads as subagents, collecting results, synthesizing) but catches genuine runaway execution. Subagent turns do NOT count against the CSO's budget -- team leads have their own maxTurns: 5.

2. Add a new section "## Timeout and Graceful Degradation" after the existing "## Disposition and Susceptibility Mitigation" section. This section should contain (~30-40 lines):

   a. **Priority instruction:** "If you have dispatched research team leads but have not received all findings, prioritize producing a complete Research Dossier with the findings you have collected. A partial dossier with honest gap reporting is more valuable than no dossier at all."

   b. **Research Gaps section template:** When producing a partial dossier, append a clearly delineated "RESEARCH GAPS" section at the end of the Research Dossier. The section must list:
      - Each team lead that did not complete (by name: e.g., "Market Intelligence Lead")
      - What intelligence they were investigating (the research sub-question they were assigned)
      - Example format:
        ```
        RESEARCH GAPS
        The following research areas were not completed:
        - Market Intelligence Lead: market sizing investigation incomplete
        - Technology Scout: technology landscape analysis incomplete
        ```

   c. **Behavioral priority:** "When you sense that you are running low on turns (e.g., you have dispatched leads but are still waiting for results), focus on: (1) synthesizing whatever team lead findings have returned, (2) listing what remains outstanding in the RESEARCH GAPS section, (3) producing the Evidence Quality Grade as 'Limited -- partial research' when gaps exist."

   d. **Do NOT instruct the CSO to count its turns.** The CSO cannot programmatically detect remaining turns. The instruction is behavioral: prioritize output completeness over output breadth.

**Part B: Update orchestration protocol (config/orchestration-protocol.md)**

1. **Phase 1.5 timeout policy:** Add a paragraph at the end of the "## Phase 1.5 -- CSO Research Directive (Conditional)" section (after the "### Dossier Broadcast" subsection, before the `---` separator). The paragraph should state:
   - The CSO operates under a maxTurns limit that constrains its total execution
   - If the CSO reaches its turn limit before completing all research, it produces a partial Research Dossier with a RESEARCH GAPS section identifying incomplete areas
   - The orchestrator should accept partial dossiers and proceed with the cascade -- partial evidence is better than blocking the entire deliberation
   - (~5-10 lines)

2. **RESEARCH STATUS flag in broadcast:** In the Phase 0 broadcast contents list (lines 28-37), add a new bullet point after the existing "The Research Dossier from the CSO" line:
   ```
   - If Phase 1.5 research was incomplete: `RESEARCH STATUS: INCOMPLETE -- gaps: [list of team leads that did not complete]` as a standalone line. Absence of this line means research was complete or CSO was not activated.
   ```
   This must be a standalone, pattern-matchable line -- not buried in prose. Per user decision: "a single line that C-suite agents can pattern-match on."
